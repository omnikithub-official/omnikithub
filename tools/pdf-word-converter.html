How to convert PDF to Word

Choose PDF to Word, upload your PDF, check the extracted text, then download it as a Word file or plain text. You can also copy or print it.

How to convert Word or text to PDF

Choose Word / Text to PDF, upload a .docx or .txt file (or type directly in the box), then download the PDF. Long documents are split across pages automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser and your documents never leave your device.

Can it read scanned PDFs?

Yes. Tick "Scanned PDF? Use OCR", choose English, Bangla or both, and the tool reads the text from the page images. Accuracy depends on scan quality.

Does it keep the layout and images?

No. PDF to Word extracts text only. Images, tables and exact layout are not kept, and scanned (image-only) PDFs have no text to extract.

Does it support Bangla?

Yes, Bangla text can be turned into a PDF. Older .doc files are not supported, so save them as .docx first.
