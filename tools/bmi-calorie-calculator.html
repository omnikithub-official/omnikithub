About BMI & Daily Calorie Calculator

Welcome to OmniKitHub's free online BMI and Calorie Calculator. This comprehensive health tool calculates your Body Mass Index to evaluate your weight status and estimates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) to guide your fitness goals.

Key Features & Health Metrics

  • Accurate BMI Evaluation: Instantly categorizes your body mass index into underweight, normal, overweight, or obese ranges.
  • TDEE & Calorie Targets: Calculates exact daily maintenance calories along with custom targets for safe weight loss or muscle gain.
  • Activity Level Adjustment: Accounts for your weekly exercise routine ranging from sedentary to highly active lifestyles.
  • 100% Free & Private: Computes health data instantly in your browser with complete privacy.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Select your gender and enter your current age, height in centimeters, and weight in kilograms.
  2. Choose your daily physical activity level from the dropdown options.
  3. Click Calculate Results to view your precise BMI score and custom calorie targets.
