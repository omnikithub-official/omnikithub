About All-in-One Financial & Loan Calculator

Welcome to OmniKitHub's comprehensive financial calculator suite. This all-in-one utility helps individuals, investors, and loan applicants estimate precise monthly EMIs, fixed deposit maturity yields, and systematic investment plan (SIP) compounding growth instantly.

Key Financial Calculators Included

  • Loan EMI Calculator: Computes monthly loan installments, total interest payable, and total loan cost based on principal, interest rate, and tenure.
  • Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator: Estimates lump-sum investment returns with compounding interest over custom deposit periods.
  • SIP Investment Growth Calculator: Projects future wealth accumulation for recurring monthly mutual fund contributions.
  • Instant Client-Side Computation: Performs fast financial modeling securely inside your web browser.

How to Use the Financial Calculator

  1. Select your preferred calculation mode using the top tabs: Loan EMI, Fixed Deposit, or SIP Growth.
  2. Enter the primary amount, annual interest rate percentage, and investment or loan duration in years.
  3. Click Calculate Returns to review your complete financial breakdown instantly.
