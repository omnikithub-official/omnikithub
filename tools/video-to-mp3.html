Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser and the file never leaves your device.

Which formats work?

MP4, WebM and MOV usually work, as do audio files like MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG. Support depends on your browser, so some AVI, MKV or WMV files may not open. If that happens, try another browser such as Chrome.

Are there size limits?

The audio is decoded in your device's memory, so very large or very long files can be slow or fail, especially on phones. For long videos, use the start and end fields to convert in parts.

MP3 or WAV?

MP3 is much smaller and works everywhere. WAV is uncompressed and keeps full quality, but the file is about ten times bigger.
