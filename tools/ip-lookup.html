What Is an IP Address?

An Internet Protocol (IP) address identifies a device or network connection on an IP network. Your public IP address is generally assigned by your internet service provider and can help identify approximate network location information.

How Does IP Lookup Work?

This free IP lookup tool requests publicly available network information from an external IP information service. Results may include your public IP address, country, city, ISP, and timezone. Location information is approximate and may not identify your exact address.
