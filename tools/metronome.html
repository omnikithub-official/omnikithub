About Pro Online Metronome & Beat Counter

OmniKitHub's Metronome is an essential free online tool for musicians, guitarists, pianists, and drummers. Maintain steady timing, improve rhythm consistency, and practice songs at precise BPM speeds.

Key Features

  • Customizable BPM: Easily adjust tempo from 30 to 250 beats per minute using precise controls or slider.
  • Tap Tempo: Tap along to any song rhythm to instantly calculate its BPM.
  • Visual Beat Indicators: Keep track of measures with highlighted dot markers for 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, and 6/8 time signatures.
  • Web Audio Powered: Crisp, lag-free synthetic click sound generated directly in your browser.
